Davit Nersisyan

I am an art and documentary photographer based in Yerevan. I have graduated from the International Center of Photography in New York and adopted a long-term project approach. That experience shaped my understanding of images as more than aesthetic objects but rather as a medium of communication through constantly evolving visual culture. During my professional career I have worked as a librarian, educator and freelance photographer being focused on transferring emotions through and above the medium.

My work focuses on people and stories that often fall outside mainstream narratives. I collaborate closely with the individuals I photograph, building relationships that allow their voices and experiences to guide the work. Over time, I began developing multi-chapter projects that combine photography, personal archives, video, and writing on intersection with contemporary technologies. One of these projects, which explores the sensory world of people with visual disabilities, received the IMAnext Japanese GrandPrix '21 and Prince Claus Seed Award in 2024.
